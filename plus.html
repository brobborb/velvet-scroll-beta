Pick up where you left off, anywhere

Set it up once. After that you don’t have to think about it.

  1. Connect once

    In Backup, choose Connect Google Drive and sign in on each device. Velvet Scroll can only see the one file it creates there.

  2. It keeps itself in step

    On your phone, tablet or computer, the book opens at your page, with your highlights and notes where you left them.

Your books stay on your devices

Sync carries your reading life, not your library files. The books themselves are never uploaded, to Google or to us.

What syncs

  • Your place in every book
  • Shelves and reading status
  • Book details and author bios
  • Highlights and notes
  • Display, appearance and accessibility settings

What doesn’t

  • The books themselves: copy them to each device once with a backup file
  • Anything to a Velvet Scroll server: the sync file lives in your own Google Drive
  • Advertising, tracking or an account with us

Prefer not to use Google? Backups and sync codes stay free, and move your places between devices by hand. See the privacy policy.

Questions

Are my books uploaded anywhere?

No. Your books stay in the browser on each of your devices. Sync only carries small things like your page, shelves, highlights and notes, in one file in your own Google Drive.

Do I need a Google account?

For automatic sync, yes, because the sync file lives in your Google Drive. Without one, the free backups and sync codes still move your places between devices by hand.

What happens when I’m offline?

Everything keeps working. Your changes are saved on your device and sync the next time you’re online.

Is it a subscription?

No. Plus is a single payment of A$15. Keep it as long as you like, with any new Plus features included.

Can I get my money back?

Yes. Ask within 30 days for a full refund, no questions asked. See the refund policy.
